Getting to the Island

The only way over is the ferry.

  1. Drive to Birchwood

    Red Cedar Lake is in northwest Wisconsin, about two hours from Minneapolis–Saint Paul. The street address is unreliable in maps apps, so search for “Stout’s Island Lodge”

    search this, not the address

    2799 27th Street, Birchwood, WI 54817

  2. Leave the car ashore

    Park in the lodge lot. A gravel path leads to the Shore House and the dock. Cars can’t come to the island.

  3. Cross the lake

    The crossing takes a few minutes. The lodge ferry usually meets the Shore House dock on the hour. We’ll post the exact schedule for our weekend here.

Where to Stay

Everyone stays on the island.

The whole weekend happens here and we want you with us for all of it. You’ll be in the main lodge or one of the cabins around it, with fires going and the lake right outside. Once you RSVP, our planner will reach out with everything you need to know about your room.

Dressed up, with room to breathe.

Saturday night is black tie, so go all out. Mornings are casual. Each evening has its own theme and we’ll share those with the invitation.

It’s October on a lake. Days are usually in the 50s and nights get close to 40.

bring a layer

It’s often breezy by the water. Bring a coat or wrap you’d be happy to be photographed in. Plan on shoes that can handle grass, gravel paths and a dock. Block heels or flats will be easiest on the lawns.

Questions

What can we answer?

How do we get to the island?

By boat. It’s the only way over and one of our favorite parts of the trip. Drive to Stout’s Island Lodge and park in the lodge lot. From there, follow the gravel path to the Shore House and the dock. The crossing only takes a few minutes. We’ll post the boat schedule for our weekend here. Give yourself a little extra time to park and settle in. Once you’re on the boat, the weekend has started.

What address do I use?

Search “Stout’s Island Lodge” in your maps app. The street address tends to send people the wrong way. For reference, it’s 2799 27th Street, Birchwood, WI 54817.

I’m flying in. Where should I land?

Fly into Minneapolis–Saint Paul (MSP). It has the most nonstop flights and plenty of rental cars. The island is a little over two hours away and a rental car is the easiest way to get here.

Where do I stay?

On the island with all of us. After you RSVP, our planner will reach out with lodging details.

Do I need to plan any meals?

No. Every meal is covered from the moment you arrive Friday until the boat takes you back Sunday. We’ll take good care of you. If you have any dietary needs, tell us on your RSVP.

Is there anything I should know about the island?
  • Cars stay on the mainland, so plan to walk the island. Paths are gravel and grass. There are also some stairs and docks.
  • If uneven ground or stairs are hard for you or someone you’re bringing, let our planner know early. We’ll make it easy.
  • The lodge is over a hundred years old, built of red cedar with stone fireplaces. It’s a little quirky and we love it for that.
Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will list everyone who’s invited.

When do I RSVP?

Formal invitations are coming. You’ll RSVP right here once yours arrives and the deadline will be printed on the invitation.

What’s the dress code?

Black tie on Saturday night and casual in the mornings. Each evening has a theme that we’ll share with the invitation. See the Attire section for more.

What’s the weather like in early October?

Usually crisp. Days are in the 50s and nights are near 40. It’s colder by the water, so pack layers.

Is the ceremony outside?

That’s the plan, by the lake. There’s a covered backup close by if the weather doesn’t cooperate.

Do I have to come to everything?

We’re building out a full weekend and we’d love for you to be there from Friday through Sunday.

What should I pack?

We’ll send you a packing list closer to the weekend. For now, think layers and shoes that can handle the outdoors.

I have another question. Who do I ask?

Our planner, Steph. She knows every detail of this weekend. Email steph@bookserce.com.
